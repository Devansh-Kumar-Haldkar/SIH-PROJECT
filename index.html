About OceanEmbed Deep Learning Subsurface Reconstructor

1. Hackathon Objective & MoES Problem Statement

Current ocean monitoring relies on sparse Argo floats (~4000 globally) leaving massive geographic blind spots. Satellites capture 100% surface observations (SST, SSH) but cannot penetrate deep water. OceanEmbed reconstructs continuous 3D temperature grids (0–2000m) from 2D satellite observations in real-time.

2. Channel-to-Depth ResU-Net Architecture

  • Input: 2D Satellite NetCDF arrays (Sea Surface Temperature θo, Sea Surface Height ζ, MLD, Salinity).
  • Encoder: Modified ResNet-18 extracting spatial mesoscale eddy features.
  • Decoder: Transposed Conv mapping 2D surface anomalies into 40 distinct depth channels.
  • Loss: Depth-weighted MSE penalizing upper-ocean errors where thermal gradients are steepest.

3. Cyclone & Ocean Heat Potential (TCHP)

The app computes integrated Tropical Cyclone Heat Potential (kJ/cm²) down to the 26°C isotherm, delivering mission-critical early warning intelligence for MoES and IMD cyclone tracking.
